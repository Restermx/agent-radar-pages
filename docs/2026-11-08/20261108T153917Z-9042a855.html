A production rule system based framework for fault root cause tracing of complex equipment driven by joint inference of fault knowledge and operational information ↗

RESS · 出版商摘要（检索索引） · 日期未提供 论文摘要

RESS 2026 年 10 月卷期，DOI：10.1016/j.ress.2026.112408。作者把故障传播知识整理为分层产生式规则，以运行数据中的异常变量启动推理，逐层追溯部件故障原因，再通过多准则决策对候选根因排序。摘要报告在海上风机传动链上进行了对比验证。该方法适合研究知识与传感数据如何联合诊断；本次未核读全文，未复核指标、消融及推广能力。

为什么值得读　把异常检测、根因定位和检修确认连起来，可用于比较纯数据驱动诊断与知识驱动诊断。

Yufan Li · Rongxi Wang · Zhen Wang · Yi Guo · Zhenyuan Chang
阅读原论文摘要 · Original abstract

Fault root cause tracing (FRCT) is crucial for the reliable operation of complex equipment. However, component-level FRCT in large complex systems remains difficult due to the multiplicity of fault modes and the reliance on experienced engineers to interpret variable-level causes. Fault-centric hybrid methods have been explored, yet existing studies still face challenges of limited scalability, insufficient utilization of operational information, and subjectivity introduced by manually designed indicators. Therefore, a fault-centric FRCT framework that integrates fault knowledge and operational information through joint inference is proposed. A production rule system with hierarchical fault-level design is constructed, with rules derived from fault propagation knowledge and supplemented by abnormal monitoring variables. An anomaly detection algorithm extracts abnormal variable information from operational data as the starting point for inference. A hierarchical inference strategy then progressively traces system-level faults to failure causes, with certainty factors and occurrence degrees quantifying fault likelihood from knowledge and data perspectives, respectively. Finally, multi-criteria decision analysis ranks candidate causes to support rapid identification when combined with on-site inspection. The framework is validated on an offshore wind turbine drivetrain through comparative experiments. Results show that it effectively supports fault-centric hybrid FRCT and enhances the efficiency and accuracy of maintenance in complex equipment.

Multivariate degradation modeling with long-term dependency for operational reliability assessment ↗

RESS · 出版商摘要（检索索引） · 日期未提供 论文摘要

RESS 2026 年 8 月卷期，DOI：10.1016/j.ress.2026.112496。研究结合分数布朗运动、个体差异和 D-vine Copula，把多个退化特征对应的边际 RUL 分布融合成系统层面的可靠性评估。作者以航空机电伺服电机验证，报告条件失效概率和分阶段可靠性曲线可为状态维修提供信息。适合关注相关退化、长期依赖和不确定性建模；摘要未给出可直接外推的工程收益。

为什么值得读　从单一 RUL 点预测走向多部件关联与系统决策，适合作为退化建模方向的阅读入口。

Heyao Song · Ningyun Lu · Bin Jiang · Wen Shi · Lihua Meng
阅读原论文摘要 · Original abstract

This paper presents a multivariate degradation modeling framework for operational reliability assessment of high-reliability aerospace systems. The methodology integrates fractional Brownian motion with unit-to-unit variability modeling to simultaneously capture long-term dependency and quantify individual uncertainties in degradation processes. A drawable vine Copula hierarchically fuses marginal remaining useful life distributions into a joint probability framework at the system level. Validated on aerospace electromechanical servomotors, the proposed framework shows that: (1) physically interpretable and physics-guided degradation parameters can provide a consistent statistical description of multi-component degradation behaviors; (2) feature-level conditional failure probabilities offer quantitative information that can be used to inform maintenance decision-making; and (3) the resulting system reliability curves exhibit stage-dependent characteristics that are consistent with different phases of degradation and may support condition-based maintenance planning. The framework aims to facilitate condition-dependent and dynamically updatable operational reliability assessment under practical constraints of data scarcity and environmental variability.

Maintenance planning for nonlinearly degrading systems: a reliability-constrained non-periodic inspection strategy ↗

RESS · 出版商摘要（检索索引） · 日期未提供 论文摘要

RESS 2026 年 11 月卷期，DOI：10.1016/j.ress.2026.112637。针对退化逐渐加速的系统，作者研究带可靠性约束的非周期检查策略，在无限时域 MDP 中联合考虑清洗、预防更换和故障更换，以最小化折扣总成本。数值案例报告其成本低于所比较的基线，且可靠性要求显著影响检查策略。这里的证据是模型分析和数值案例，不能直接当作现场节省比例。

为什么值得读　把寿命与可靠性预测转化为“何时检查、何时换件”的决策，是从 RUL 模型走向维护优化的重要连接。

Ziyu Wang · Xiujie Zhao · Shuguang He
阅读原论文摘要 · Original abstract

Manufacturing systems are prone to gradual degradation, which, subject to uncertainties from internal physical mechanisms and external environmental disturbances, typically manifests as stochastic variability. Such stochastic degradation processes exhibit temporal nonlinearity, with degradation rates evolving from slow to accelerated over time. Traditional Condition-Based Maintenance (CBM) frameworks primarily rely on periodic inspections, which fail to capture the acceleration characteristics of nonlinear degradation processes. To address this limitation, this paper investigates a CBM optimization problem for nonlinearly degrading systems under a reliability-constrained non-periodic inspection policy. Three maintenance actions are considered: cleaning, preventive replacement, and corrective replacement. The maintenance decision problem is formulated as an infinite-horizon Markov Decision Process (MDP), with the objective of minimizing the total discounted cost. Critical structural properties of the optimal inspection strategy and the optimal maintenance policy are analytically established. Numerical examples are provided to validate the effectiveness of the proposed model. Results show that the proposed model achieves the lowest discounted cost compared to benchmark models. Sensitivity analyses reveal that reliability constraints have a more pronounced impact on the optimal inspection strategy than inspection costs, with stricter reliability requirements leading to higher costs.

PEFT-MuTS: A Multivariate Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning Framework for Remaining Useful Life Prediction based on Cross-domain Time Series Representation Model ↗

arXiv · 作者公开预印本 v2（非期刊终稿） · 2026-01-30 论文摘要

作者公开预印本，2026-01-30 首次提交、2026-08-14 更新为 v2；本条依据 arXiv 摘要，不等同于 RESS 期刊终稿。PEFT-MuTS 利用跨领域时间序列预训练表示，通过特征适配、低秩多变量融合和零初始化回归器进行少样本 RUL 预测。作者报告在航空发动机、工业轴承数据上使用少于 1% 的目标设备样本仍有效，并提供代码。阅读时应进一步核查数据切分、少样本定义和跨设备迁移设置。

为什么值得读　将预训练与参数高效微调引入 PHM，适合连接你已有的 AI 兴趣与寿命预测研究。

En Fu · Yanyan Hu · Zengwang Jin · Kaixiang Peng
阅读原论文摘要 · Original abstract

The application of data-driven remaining useful life (RUL) prediction has long been constrained by the availability of large amount of degradation data. Mainstream solutions such as domain adaptation and meta-learning still rely on large amounts of historical degradation data from equipment that is identical or similar to the target, which imposes significant limitations in practical applications. This study investigates PEFT-MuTS, a Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning framework for few-shot RUL prediction, built on cross-domain pre-trained time-series representation models. Contrary to the widely held view that knowledge transfer in RUL prediction can only occur within similar devices, we demonstrate that substantial benefits can be achieved through pre-training process with large-scale cross-domain time series datasets. A independent feature tuning network and a meta-variable-based low rank multivariate fusion mechanism are developed to enable the pre-trained univariate time-series representation backbone model to fully exploit the multivariate relationships in degradation data for downstream RUL prediction task. Additionally, we introduce a zero-initialized regressor that stabilizes the fine-tuning process under few-shot conditions. Experiments on aero-engine and industrial bearing datasets demonstrate that our method can achieve effective RUL prediction even when less than 1\% of samples of target equipment are used. Meanwhile, it substantially outperforms conventional supervised and few-shot approaches while markedly reducing the data required to achieve high predictive accuracy. Our code is available at https://github.com/fuen1590/PEFT-MuTS.
